How It Works

Seamless end-to-end custody and verification in 3 simple steps

1. Report with Secret Clue

Submit details about what was lost or found. Add a confidential verification clue that only the genuine owner knows. Secret clues stay 100% hidden from the public.

2. Smart Match Engine

Our algorithm compares categories, GPS & building zones, date proximities, color, brand, and description TF-IDF similarity to score potential matches out of 100%.

3. Verify & Handover

Claimants submit ownership proof. Reviewers inspect claimant answers against hidden details side by side, schedule returns on the calendar, and log signed handovers.
